Ukraine's dismissed defence minister Fedorov publicly calls for wartime elections

Mykhailo Fedorov, 35, dismissed as defence minister in July amid friction with military leadership, posted a nine-minute video on 18 August demanding Ukraine "restore a full democratic process even amid a prolonged war." As of Wednesday afternoon Zelenskyy had not directly responded, though he has previously said he would be open to elections if security could be guaranteed, asking the United States and European partners for help securing a vote. Experts note major practical obstacles regardless of political will: Russia occupies roughly 19% of Ukraine, home to an estimated 4.5 million adults, and millions more Ukrainians are internally displaced; any vote would need a ceasefire guarantee Kyiv does not currently trust Moscow to honour.

Iran / Hormuz

UAE suspends all trade with Iran over the 18 Aug missile alert; Iran calls the claim a false flag

The UAE's Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced that "all trade, commercial exchanges and financial transactions with Iran have been halted until further notice," citing "escalations that undermine peace and security in the region" — a direct reference to the two ballistic missiles the UAE Ministry of Defence said targeted its waters on 18 August. It is the first Gulf-state economic break with Iran since the war began.

Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei rejected the accusation as baseless, calling it a "false flag operation" that contradicts the two countries' neighbourly relations, and urged regional parties to avoid unfounded allegations. This directly opposes the UAE Ministry of Defence's own account, already carried in Brief 009 as attributed-claim, not verified-primary. Neither government's account is independently verified by a third party in this sweep; this ledger carries both rather than adjudicating.

So what The first Gulf-state economic break with Iran this war is real and concrete regardless of who is right about the missiles — rhetoric and a real diplomatic act now point the same direction. IR holds at 9, escalating — deliberately: 10 remains reserved for an actual hit against the US, the UAE or Iran, and a trade suspension, however serious, is not yet that.

UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs statement via Al Jazeera (fetched in full, 03:34 UTC 19 August), cross-checked against CNN, Bloomberg, Euronews, The Washington Post, Breitbart, EgyptToday and The National — the suspension itself is attributed-claim, well-corroborated. Iran's denial via the same Al Jazeera report. The underlying missile-launch claim is contested between the two governments.

02
Russia / Ukraine

Kherson and Zaporizhzhia drone strikes kill at least six, wound twelve

A Russian first-person-view drone hit a minibus in Kherson at roughly 8:50am local time (05:50 GMT), killing at least four and wounding four more; a separate drone strike hit a car in the same city, killing one and wounding one. Kherson Governor Oleksandr Prokudin, who reported both strikes, described a broader pattern of Russian attacks on civilians in the city. A separate Russian drone strike on Zaporizhzhia, reported by regional governor Ivan Fedorov — no relation to Mykhailo Fedorov, the dismissed defence minister in the item above — killed one and injured seven. Russia's Defence Ministry said it downed 453 Ukrainian drones overnight across various regions.

So what In line with the thread's recent pattern rather than a new escalation — which is why RU holds at 8, volatile for the second straight brief despite a real and rising civilian toll.

Kherson Governor Oleksandr Prokudin and Zaporizhzhia Governor Ivan Fedorov, via Al Jazeera Staff/AFP/Reuters (fetched in full, 09:34 UTC 19 August) and UPI. attributed-claim — regional officials' own accounts of strikes on their territory, not independently verified by a third party. The headline toll was revised upward from an earlier count of five during reporting.
