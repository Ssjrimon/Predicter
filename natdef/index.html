Bottom line — brief 13, 2026-09-06
The war restarted. After about a month without a direct exchange the US struck Iran on 1 September, citing a failed Iranian attempt to mine the strait and hit a US base in Jordan, and Iran retaliated across five countries hosting US facilities — Kuwait, Bahrain, the UAE, Jordan and Iraq. The Qatari mediation channel that opened Brief 012's window is dead; Vance says there will be no talks until Iran stops attacking commercial shipping. Iran alleges a US strike killed civilians at a wedding in Sirik: the figures differ between outlets, Vance is 'extremely skeptical', CENTCOM says it does not target civilians, and the claim runs here unresolved. Separately, the one number everyone uses to judge whether Hormuz works is now itself in dispute — Treasury, Energy and the White House cite 10 to 18 million barrels a day while Lloyd's List Intelligence counts about 14 non-Iranian-linked ships a day and JMIC's 1 September advisory calls traffic far below baseline. Brent closed the week near $96, its strongest since mid-July. On Russia, the first reciprocal restraint of the war: Putin ordered a 72-hour pause on Kyiv, Zelenskyy reciprocated on Moscow, and Witkoff and Kushner met Putin for over three hours on 5 September and Zelenskyy on 6 September, with no breakthrough announced by any party. IR moves to 10 and escalating; EU to 6 after Germany formally blamed Russia for the Leipzig airport drone attack.
